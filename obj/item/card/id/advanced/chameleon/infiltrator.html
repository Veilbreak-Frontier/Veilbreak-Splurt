infiltrator
/obj/item/card/id/advanced/chameleon/infiltrator

Agent card issued to Syndicate infiltration operatives. Can only forge low-ranking cover roles, and can swap between its cover identity and the holder's real identity via AltClick (syndicate-only).

Vars

current_disguise_trimThe currently applied disguise trim path, tracked so the toggle can round-trip the cover.
hidden_identitySnapshot of the alternate identity. AltClick swaps the card's displayed identity with this one.

Procs

is_cover_identityTRUE when this card is currently presenting its cover (fake Nanotrasen) identity rather than the real (hidden) Syndicate identity.
prime_real_identityMarks the card's current access as always-active hidden access. Called on spawn for infiltration operatives so the syndicate access survives retrims. The real identity snapshot is captured lazily on the first forge, when the card's registered name and account are guaranteed to be populated.
toggle_identitySwap the card between its cover identity and its real (hidden) identity.
